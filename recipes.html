Recipes from the grill and the rice pot

Five dependable dishes we cook again and again: smoky, fresh and easy to make at home, whether you have a charcoal grill or just a hot pan.

Vegan · 30 minutes

Blistered edamame rice bowl

Serves 2 · 30 minutes

Our everyday bowl: smoky edamame, crisp vegetables and a punchy ginger-scallion sauce over warm rice.

Ingredients

  • 300 g edamame in pods (or 150 g shelled)
  • 2 portions cooked short-grain rice
  • 1 carrot and ½ cucumber, cut into matchsticks
  • 2 spring onions and a thumb of ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil

Method

  1. Grill or pan-fry the edamame over high heat until blistered; season with salt.
  2. Finely chop the spring onions and ginger, then mix with soy, vinegar and sesame oil.
  3. Divide rice into bowls, top with vegetables and shelled edamame, and spoon over the sauce.
Vegan · 25 minutes

Soba with grilled shiitake

Serves 2 · 25 minutes

Nutty buckwheat noodles in a light, savoury broth, topped with glazed, smoky mushrooms.

Ingredients

  • 180 g dried soba noodles
  • 200 g shiitake mushrooms
  • 700 ml vegetable stock with a strip of kombu
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Spring onions and toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Simmer the stock and kombu for 10 minutes, remove the kombu and season with soy.
  2. Grill the shiitake until tender, brushing with soy and maple syrup at the end.
  3. Cook the soba, rinse briefly, then serve in the hot broth topped with mushrooms and spring onions.
Vegetarian · 20 minutes

Miso-butter grilled corn

Serves 4 · 20 minutes

Sweetcorn and miso are a perfect match: sweet, salty and smoky all at once.

Ingredients

  • 4 cobs of sweetcorn
  • 40 g soft butter or plant-based spread
  • 1 tbsp white miso
  • 1 lime
  • Shichimi or chilli flakes and chopped coriander

Method

  1. Mash the butter with the miso and a little lime zest.
  2. Grill the corn over medium heat for 10–15 minutes, turning until charred in spots.
  3. Brush generously with miso butter, then finish with lime juice, chilli and coriander.
Vegan · 15 minutes

Silken tofu with ginger-scallion oil

Serves 2 · 15 minutes

A cool, silky starter or light supper that comes together with almost no cooking.

Ingredients

  • 300 g silken tofu
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 thumb of ginger, finely grated
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1–2 tbsp soy sauce and toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Gently warm or steam the tofu if you like it warm, then place on a plate.
  2. Put the spring onions and ginger in a heatproof bowl; heat the oil until shimmering and carefully pour over.
  3. Stir in soy sauce, spoon over the tofu and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
Pescatarian · 30 minutes

Soy-ginger grilled fish with greens

Serves 4 · 30 minutes

A simple marinade of soy, ginger and citrus turns any firm fish fillet into a weeknight treat.

Ingredients

  • 4 firm fish fillets, such as salmon or cod
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 thumb of ginger, grated, and 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon or lime
  • Greens such as pak choi or green beans

Method

  1. Marinate the fish in soy, honey, ginger, garlic and citrus juice for 15 minutes.
  2. Grill over medium heat, skin-side down first, until the fish flakes easily and reaches 63°C (145°F) inside.
  3. Grill or steam the greens and serve with rice and lemon wedges.

Food safety

Cook fish until opaque and it reaches 63°C (145°F). Keep raw and cooked foods separate on the grill.

Allergens

Soy, sesame, wheat (in many soy sauces) and fish are common allergens. Use tamari for a wheat-free option.

No grill?

A very hot griddle pan, a heavy frying pan or your oven grill gives great results for every recipe.
