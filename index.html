The pantry

Six staples, endless dinners

With these six ingredients in the cupboard, a bowl of rice and a handful of vegetables become a satisfying meal in minutes.

Soy sauce

Salty, savoury backbone of most marinades and glazes. Choose naturally brewed for the best flavour.

Miso

Fermented soybean paste. White miso is mild and sweet; red miso is deeper and saltier.

Fresh ginger

Grated into dressings, sauces and marinades for warmth and brightness.

Nori

Toasted seaweed sheets for rice balls and bowls, or crumbled over as a savoury topping.

Sesame

Toasted seeds and sesame oil add nutty depth; a few drops of oil go a long way.

Matcha

Stone-ground green tea powder for drinks, desserts and a gentle savoury salt.

Cooking over embers

Four rules for the charcoal grill

The best grilling happens over glowing embers, not leaping flames. Patience at the start rewards you with steady, even heat.

  1. Wait for grey. Cook once the coals are covered in a light grey ash.

  2. Build zones. Pile coals on one side for high heat and leave the other side cooler.

  3. Clean and oil. A hot, clean, lightly oiled grate stops food sticking.

  4. Glaze late. Sweet soy or miso glazes burn quickly, so brush them on at the end.

Weeknight favourite

Miso-glazed vegetable skewers

Thread chunks of courgette, shiitake, spring onion and firm tofu onto soaked bamboo skewers. Grill over medium heat for 8–10 minutes, turning often, then brush with a glaze of white miso, a little honey or maple syrup, rice vinegar and grated ginger for the final two minutes.

Serve over steamed rice with a handful of blistered edamame and a sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds.

Ask the kitchen
What is edamame?

Edamame are young, green soybeans harvested before they mature. They are usually boiled, steamed or grilled in their pods and seasoned with salt. Only the beans inside are eaten; the tough pods are discarded.

Is edamame good on the grill?

Yes. Grilling the pods over high heat blisters and chars them, adding a smoky flavour. A grill basket or perforated tray stops them falling through the grate.

Do I need a charcoal grill for these recipes?

No. A gas grill, stovetop griddle pan or oven grill works for every recipe. Charcoal simply adds extra smokiness.

What is umami?

Umami is the savoury taste found in foods such as miso, soy sauce, mushrooms, seaweed and ripe tomatoes. Balancing it with acidity and freshness makes dishes taste rounded.

Are your recipes vegetarian?

Most are plant-forward, and many are fully vegetarian or vegan. A few include fish or eggs. Each recipe is clearly labelled, and we suggest swaps where possible.

Is Edamame Cinder a restaurant?

No. Edamame Cinder is an independent home-cooking website. We publish recipes and guides; we do not operate a restaurant, sell food or take orders.
