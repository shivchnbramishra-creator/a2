About Edamame Cinder

A home-cooking website for people who love vegetables, rice, a little smoke and food that feels both comforting and light.

Our story

Edamame on the grill

Edamame Cinder started on a summer evening when a bag of frozen edamame ended up in a grill basket over glowing coals. Blistered, smoky and sprinkled with salt, they disappeared in minutes.

That simple discovery led to years of cooking Japanese-inspired dishes at home: rice bowls, noodle soups, glazed vegetables and quick pickles. This website collects the recipes and techniques we rely on most.

Tested at home

Every recipe is cooked in an ordinary home kitchen, often several times, before we publish it.

Plant-forward

Vegetables and grains come first, with fish and eggs as occasional additions, clearly labelled.

Respectful

We are inspired by Japanese home cooking and describe our recipes honestly as inspired, not traditional.

How we work

From test kitchen to your table

We write recipes with ingredients you can find in most large supermarkets and Asian grocers, and we suggest substitutes where helpful. Cooking temperatures and storage advice follow recognised food safety guidance.
